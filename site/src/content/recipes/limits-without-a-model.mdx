Checking a measurement against a limit is a comparison. Yield is a count. Cpk is a mean, a standard deviation and a subtraction. A control chart is those numbers plotted against time. Grouping the same measurements by lot, by fixture, by day and by shift is a `GROUP BY`. None of it needs a model, and the pass or fail decision must never involve one: a wrong pass ships a bad unit.
