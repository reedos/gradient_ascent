Five prototype boards come back from the board house and nobody knows yet what the design does at
the edges of its own specification. A sweep over input voltage, load current and ambient
temperature is a nested loop. The margin to a datasheet limit at each of those corners is a
subtraction. The uncertainty that says whether a margin is real is a root sum of squares over four
named numbers, and guardbanding a limit by it is another subtraction. No model is involved
anywhere, and none should be: a model never produces a reported measurement, an uncertainty, a
margin or a verdict.
