Writing a driver means reading a programming manual and turning it into commands. Instruments that all claim SCPI compliance still differ on keyword form, on whether a boolean is `ON` or `1`, and on whether a reading comes back with a unit suffix, so a command that works on one instrument is not evidence about another.
