# Halvorsen Parts List

Applies to: DW-300, DW-480, DR-210, DR-520. Revision 2025-08-01. Prices are in U.S. dollars and
do not include labor.

## 1. How to Order

Order by part number through an authorized Halvorsen service center. Have the model number,
serial number, and proof of purchase ready; some parts are model-specific and the wrong part
number cannot be returned once installed. A service technician can also confirm a part number
by inspecting the unit if the owner is unsure which part is needed. Special orders for a
discontinued part can take up to three weeks; a common part such as a filter or a belt usually
ships within two business days.

## 5. Pricing and Availability Notes

Prices are current as of the revision date above and are subject to change without notice.
Contact an authorized service center to confirm current pricing and availability before
ordering. Two parts in this list, HLV-2205 and HLV-7734, do not list a fitment here; each is
confirmed for a specific model in that model's owner's manual. Whether a part is covered under
warranty, and for how long, is a separate question answered by `warranty-policy.md`; this list
gives price and fitment only, not coverage.
