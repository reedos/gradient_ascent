# Halvorsen Installation Guide

Applies to: DW-300, DW-480, DR-210, DR-520. Revision 2023-09-01. Model-specific detail lives in
each model's own manual; this guide covers the rules that are the same across the line.

## 1. Before You Begin

Confirm the installation location is level, within 1/4 inch across the footprint, before running
any electrical or plumbing connections. A built-in dishwasher needs a cutout at least 24 inches
wide; a dryer needs at least 4 inches behind it for the vent connection.

## 2. Electrical Requirements

Dishwashers (DW-300, DW-480) require a dedicated 120V, 15A grounded circuit. Dryers (DR-210,
DR-520 electric) require a dedicated 240V, 30A circuit and a 4-wire power cord, which is not
included with the unit and must match local code. Do not use an extension cord or an adapter
plug with any Halvorsen appliance.

## 3. Dishwasher Water Supply and Drain

Connect the hot water supply line to a shutoff valve rated for 20 to 120 psi at up to 120°F. The
drain hose from the dishwasher to the drain connection (an air gap, a high loop, or a garbage
disposal inlet) must not exceed 8 feet in length. A high loop, where used instead of an air gap,
must reach between 20 and 34 inches above the floor.

## 4. Dryer Venting

All Halvorsen dryers vent through 4-inch rigid metal duct only; flexible foil or plastic duct is
not permitted. The general rule is a maximum total vent run of 35 feet, with up to 4 elbows
counted against that length. Always confirm the current maximum against the latest service
bulletin for the specific model before installation: a bulletin issued after this guide
supersedes both this guide and the owner's manual for that model.

## 5. Clearances and Leveling

Recheck level after the unit is connected and before first use. An unlevel dishwasher can leak
at the door gasket; an unlevel dryer can walk across the floor during the Heavy Duty cycle.

## 6. Final Checks

Run one Rinse Only cycle on a new dishwasher and one Air Fluff cycle on a new dryer before
regular use, to confirm the connections hold and the unit powers on correctly. Keep the
installation guide and the owner's manual together for future reference.
