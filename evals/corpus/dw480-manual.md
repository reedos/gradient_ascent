# Halvorsen DW-480 Owner's Manual

Family: Halvorsen Crestline. Revision 2024-01-15. This manual covers the DW-480 built-in
dishwasher only. For the entry-level DW-300, see `dw300-manual.md`.

## 1. Overview

The DW-480 is a 14 place setting built-in dishwasher, introduced in 2024 as the step-up model in
the Halvorsen Crestline family. It has three racks, including a dedicated utensil rack above the
upper rack, which the DW-300 does not have. The DW-480 does not include Wi-Fi connectivity.

## 2. Specifications

Exterior dimensions are 24 inches wide, 24 inches deep, and 34 inches tall, the same footprint as
the DW-300. Sound level during the Normal cycle is 44 dBA, measured under the same internal test
protocol used for the DW-300. The DW-480 requires a dedicated 120V, 15A grounded circuit, the
same electrical requirement as the DW-300.

## 3. Cycles

The DW-480 offers six cycles: Normal (100 minutes), Light (70 minutes), Heavy (140 minutes),
Rinse Only (15 minutes), Quick Wash (30 minutes), and Sanitize (145 minutes). The Normal cycle
uses 3.0 gallons of water. Halvorsen's annual energy estimate for the DW-480 is 240 kWh per year
under typical use of one Normal cycle per day.

## 4. Installation Requirements

Installation follows the same general rules as the DW-300; see `installation-guide.md`. The
DW-480 needs its own dedicated 120V, 15A circuit and a hot water supply between 20 and 120 psi.

## 5. Leak Cutoff Protection

The DW-480 includes a leak cutoff, a float sensor in the base pan that detects standing water. If
the leak cutoff trips, the unit shuts off its water supply and stops the cycle immediately, and
the control panel shows error code E5. The DW-300 does not have this feature.

## 6. Maintenance and Filter Cleaning

The DW-480 uses a self-cleaning filter that is rinsed automatically during each cycle. Under
normal household use, no routine manual cleaning of the filter is required. If drainage slows,
check the drain hose for kinks before assuming the filter needs attention.

## 7. Troubleshooting

See the dishwasher error codes in `troubleshooting.md`. All five dishwasher codes, E1 through
E5, can appear on the DW-480, including E5 from the leak cutoff sensor described in section 5.

## 8. Replacement Parts

The DW-480 heating element is a 900W unit, part number HLV-4472. The DW-480 door latch switch is
part number HLV-7702. The DW-480 drain pump is part number HLV-2205, available through
authorized service. Current prices for these parts are listed in `parts-list.md`.

## 9. Warranty and Safety

The DW-480 is covered by Halvorsen's standard warranty; see `warranty-policy.md` for full terms
and exclusions. Do not remove the rating label. Keep children away from detergent and the open
door during loading.
