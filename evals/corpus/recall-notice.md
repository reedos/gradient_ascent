# Recall Notice RC-2026-02

Issued 2026-05-15 by Halvorsen Technical Services. Subject: power cord defect on certain DR-210
units.

## 1. Summary

Halvorsen has identified a wiring defect in the power cord supplied with a limited range of
DR-210 dryers. The defect can cause the cord to overheat at the plug end under sustained heavy
load. The defect was traced to a single supplier batch used during the affected production
window and does not appear in DR-210 units built outside that window, or in any DR-520 unit.

## 2. Affected Units

This recall covers DR-210 units manufactured from January through March 2023, identified by a
serial number beginning with 23A01, 23A02, or 23A03. Units manufactured before or after this
window are not affected. The serial number is printed on a label inside the door frame, as noted
in `dr210-manual.md`.

## 3. Hazard

An affected cord can overheat at the plug end, which is a fire hazard if the unit is run
unattended or for long periods on Heavy Duty cycles. No injuries have been reported to
Halvorsen in connection with this defect; the recall is precautionary, based on cords returned
for inspection after customers reported warmth at the plug.

## 4. Remedy

Halvorsen will replace the power cord on an affected unit at no charge, installed by an
authorized service center. This replacement is provided regardless of the unit's warranty
status; the 2-year full warranty and the recall remedy are separate.

## 5. How to Check

Check the serial number against the affected range in section 2. If it falls within that range,
contact Halvorsen customer support or an authorized service center to schedule the free cord
replacement. Continue to use the dryer normally until the appointment, but avoid unattended long
cycles in the meantime. The appointment itself takes about twenty minutes, since only the cord
is replaced; no other part of the DR-210 is affected by this recall, and the replacement does
not change the unit's warranty start date.
