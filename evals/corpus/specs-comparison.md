# Halvorsen Specifications Comparison

Applies to: DW-300, DW-480, DR-210, DR-520. Revision 2025-08-01. This document collects
specifications from the four owner's manuals in one place for comparison and gives the rate
assumptions used in Halvorsen's own annual cost estimates.

## 1. Purpose and Assumptions

The annual cost estimates in this document, and the annual energy estimates in each
owner's manual, assume a national average electricity rate of $0.14 per kWh and a national
average water rate of $0.010 per gallon. Actual cost depends on local utility rates and how the
unit is used.

## 2. Dishwasher Specifications

The DW-300 offers 12 place settings, runs at 52 dBA, uses an estimated 260 kWh per year, and
uses 3.2 gallons of water per Normal cycle. The DW-480 offers 14 place settings, runs at 44 dBA,
uses an estimated 240 kWh per year, and uses 3.0 gallons of water per Normal cycle. Both share
the same 24 by 24 by 34 inch footprint.

## 3. Dryer Specifications

The DR-210 has a 7.0 cubic foot drum and weighs 110 lb; it is electric only. The DR-520 has a 7.8
cubic foot drum and weighs 125 lb in its electric version or 128 lb in its gas version. Halvorsen
does not rate either dryer's noise level in decibels; no dBA figure is published for the DR-210
or the DR-520.

## 4. Sample Annual Cost Calculation

As a worked example: the DW-300's estimated annual electricity cost is its annual energy estimate
of 260 kWh multiplied by the assumed rate of $0.14 per kWh, or $36.40 per year. The same method
applies to any model's kWh figure and to water cost using the gallons-per-cycle figure and the
assumed water rate.

## 5. How These Numbers Are Measured

All figures in this document and in the owner's manuals come from Halvorsen's internal test
protocol under controlled lab conditions and are estimates, not a guarantee of the cost or noise
level any individual household will experience.
