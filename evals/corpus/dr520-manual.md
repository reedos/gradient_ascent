# Halvorsen DR-520 Owner's Manual

Family: Halvorsen Crestline. Revision 2024-03-01. This manual covers the DR-520 dryer, available
in electric and gas versions. For the entry-level DR-210, see `dr210-manual.md`.

## 1. Overview

The DR-520 is a 7.8 cubic foot dryer, introduced in 2024 as the step-up model in the Halvorsen
Crestline family. It is available as an electric model or a gas model; the gas model requires
professional installation of the gas line and is not covered by the electrical sections below.
The DR-520 does not have Wi-Fi connectivity.

## 2. Specifications

The electric DR-520 requires a dedicated 240V, 30A circuit and a 4-wire power cord, not included.
The electric model weighs 125 lb empty; the gas model weighs 128 lb empty.

## 3. Cycles

The DR-520 offers seven cycles: Normal (42 minutes), Delicate (28 minutes), Heavy Duty (58
minutes), Air Fluff (20 minutes), Timed Dry (adjustable), Steam Refresh (20 minutes), and
Sanitize (55 minutes). Steam Refresh is meant to relax wrinkles in already-dry garments, not to
replace a full drying cycle.

## 4. Venting Requirements

As originally published, the DR-520 vents through 4-inch rigid metal duct with a maximum total
vent run of 35 feet and up to 4 elbows, the same general rule as the DR-210 and the installation
guide. Always check for a newer service bulletin before relying on this figure; a bulletin can
supersede it for a specific model.

## 5. Moisture Sensor and Reverse Tumble

The DR-520 has a moisture sensor and a reverse tumble action that alternates drum direction to
reduce tangling. Reverse tumble runs on every cycle except Air Fluff.

## 6. Troubleshooting

If the control panel displays an error code, see the dryer error codes in `troubleshooting.md`.
All five dryer codes, F1 through F5, can appear on the DR-520.

## 7. Replacement Parts

The DR-520 electric heating element is a 5400W unit, part number HLV-5520. The DR-520 drive belt
is part number HLV-6601, the same part used on the DR-210. The DR-520 door latch switch is part
number HLV-7735, a different part from the DR-210's latch switch. Current prices for these parts
are listed in `parts-list.md`.

## 8. Warranty and Safety

The DR-520 is covered by Halvorsen's standard warranty; see `warranty-policy.md` for full terms
and exclusions. Never operate the dryer with the lint filter removed. On gas models, do not
attempt to service the gas line yourself.
