# Halvorsen Warranty Policy

Applies to: DW-300, DW-480, DR-210, DR-520. Revision 2025-01-01. Applies to the original
purchaser only, for units purchased and used in the United States and Canada.

## 1. Full Warranty

Halvorsen covers parts and labor in full for 2 years from the original date of purchase, for
defects in materials or workmanship, on all four models covered by this policy. Proof of
purchase is required for any claim.

## 2. Limited Warranty

After the 2-year full warranty period ends, a limited warranty continues on specific major parts,
parts only, labor not included: 5 years from purchase on the dishwasher motor and tub (DW-300,
DW-480), and 7 years from purchase on the dryer drum and motor (DR-210, DR-520). No other parts
are covered after year 2.

## 3. Commercial and Rental Use

If a unit is used commercially, or installed in a rental property, the warranty is limited to 90
days from the date of purchase, regardless of the full or limited warranty terms in sections 1
and 2.

## 4. Unauthorized Repair or Modification

Any repair, part substitution, or modification performed by anyone other than an authorized
Halvorsen service provider voids the remainder of the warranty, for both the full and limited
periods.

## 5. Normal Wear Parts

Filters, belts, gaskets, and light bulbs are considered normal wear parts. These are covered for
90 days from the date of purchase only, even during the 2-year full warranty period.

## 6. What Is Not Covered

The warranty does not cover damage from installation errors, misuse, power surges or voltage
irregularities, acts of nature, or cosmetic damage that does not affect function. It does not
cover the cost of the service call if no defect is found.

## 7. How to Get Service

Contact an authorized Halvorsen service center with proof of purchase and the model and serial
number. The warranty is not transferable to a subsequent owner. Out-of-warranty repairs may be
billed at the service center's standard rate.
