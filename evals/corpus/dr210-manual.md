# Halvorsen DR-210 Owner's Manual

Family: Halvorsen Everyday. Revision 2026-05-20, which added section 8. This manual covers the
DR-210 electric dryer only. For the higher-capacity DR-520, see `dr520-manual.md`.

## 1. Overview

The DR-210 is a 7.0 cubic foot electric dryer, introduced in 2023 as the entry model in the
Halvorsen Everyday family. It is electric only; Halvorsen does not make a gas version of the
DR-210. It does not have a steam cycle and does not have Wi-Fi connectivity.

## 2. Specifications

The DR-210 requires a dedicated 240V, 30A circuit and a 4-wire power cord, which is not included
and must be purchased separately to match local code. The unit weighs 110 lb empty.

## 3. Cycles

The DR-210 offers five cycles: Normal (45 minutes), Delicate (30 minutes), Heavy Duty (60
minutes), Air Fluff (20 minutes), and Timed Dry (adjustable from 10 to 60 minutes).

## 4. Venting Requirements

The DR-210 vents through 4-inch rigid metal duct. The maximum total vent run is 35 feet, with up
to 4 elbows in the run, following the general venting rule in `installation-guide.md`. This
figure has not been revised for the DR-210.

## 5. Lint Filter and Moisture Sensor

Clean the lint filter before every load; a clogged lint filter is the leading cause of long dry
times. The DR-210 has a moisture sensor that automatically ends the cycle once the load reaches
the selected dryness level, reducing over-drying.

## 6. Troubleshooting

If the control panel displays an error code, see the dryer error codes in `troubleshooting.md`.
All five dryer codes, F1 through F5, can appear on the DR-210.

## 7. Replacement Parts

The DR-210 heating element is a 5200W unit, part number HLV-5510. The DR-210 drive belt is part
number HLV-6601, which also fits the DR-520. The door latch switch used on the DR-210 is part
number HLV-7734. Current prices for these parts are listed in `parts-list.md`.

## 8. Recall Notice

Units manufactured January through March 2023 are covered by Recall Notice RC-2026-02 for a
power cord defect that can cause overheating. See `recall-notice.md` for the affected serial
number range and the remedy. The serial number is printed on a label inside the door frame.

## 9. Warranty and Safety

The DR-210 is covered by Halvorsen's standard warranty; see `warranty-policy.md` for full terms
and exclusions. Never operate the dryer with the lint filter removed. Keep the area around the
dryer clear of lint buildup.
