# Halvorsen Care and Cleaning Guide

Applies to: DW-300, DW-480, DR-210, DR-520. Revision 2025-03-01. This guide summarizes routine
care; model-specific parts are listed in each owner's manual.

## 1. Dishwasher Filter Cleaning

The DW-300 uses a manual fine filter and should be cleaned every 30 cycles, as described in
`dw300-manual.md`. The DW-480 uses a self-cleaning filter and needs no routine manual cleaning
under normal use, as described in `dw480-manual.md`. If either unit's drainage slows, check the
drain hose before assuming the filter is the cause.

## 2. Dishwasher Deep Clean

Once a month, run an empty Heavy cycle with a dishwasher-safe cleaner to remove mineral buildup
and odor from the tub and spray arms. Do this on both the DW-300 and the DW-480. Skipping the
monthly deep clean does not void the warranty, but it is the most common reason a unit under
warranty is inspected and found to have no defect, which is a service call the warranty does
not cover.

## 3. Dryer Lint Filter

Clean the lint filter before every load on both the DR-210 and the DR-520. A partially clogged
filter is the most common cause of a long Normal cycle and can eventually trigger error code F5.

## 4. Dryer Vent Cleaning

Have the exterior dryer vent professionally cleaned once a year, more often in a household that
dries large loads daily. The universal dryer vent cleaning kit, part HLV-9002, is intended for
the section of duct between the dryer and the wall for owners who clean it themselves.

## 5. Hard Water Care

In areas with water hardness over 7 grains per gallon, run a dishwasher descaling treatment once
a month on either dishwasher model to prevent mineral buildup on the heating element and spray
arms.

## 6. Exterior and Gasket Care

Wipe the dishwasher door gasket weekly with a damp cloth to prevent odor and mildew. Wipe the
dryer drum opening and door seal after heavy lint-producing loads such as towels or blankets.
None of the routine care in this guide requires a tool beyond a soft cloth and, for the monthly
deep clean, a dishwasher-safe cleaner; no step here calls for disassembling either appliance.
