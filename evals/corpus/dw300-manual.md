# Halvorsen DW-300 Owner's Manual

Family: Halvorsen Everyday. Revision 2023-11-01. This manual covers the DW-300 built-in
dishwasher only. For the higher-capacity DW-480, see `dw480-manual.md`.

## 1. Overview

The DW-300 is a 12 place setting built-in dishwasher, introduced in 2023 as the entry model in
the Halvorsen Everyday family. It has two racks: a lower rack for plates and pots, and an upper
rack that adjusts to three heights for tall items. The DW-300 does not include a third utensil
rack, a leak cutoff sensor, or Wi-Fi connectivity; those are not part of this model.

## 2. Specifications

Exterior dimensions are 24 inches wide, 24 inches deep, and 34 inches tall. Sound level during
the Normal cycle is 52 dBA, measured under Halvorsen's internal test protocol. The DW-300
requires a dedicated 120V, 15A grounded circuit within 6 feet of the installation location. The
hot water inlet accepts supply pressure between 20 and 120 psi at a maximum inlet temperature of
120°F.

## 3. Cycles

The DW-300 offers five cycles: Normal (110 minutes), Light (75 minutes), Heavy (130 minutes),
Rinse Only (15 minutes), and Quick Wash (30 minutes). The Normal cycle uses 3.2 gallons of water.
Halvorsen's annual energy estimate for the DW-300 is 260 kWh per year under typical use of one
Normal cycle per day.

## 4. Installation Requirements

Installation must follow the general electrical and plumbing rules in `installation-guide.md`.
The DW-300 needs its own 15A circuit; it may not share a circuit with a garbage disposal or
another appliance. The drain hose from the DW-300 to the drain connection must not exceed 8 feet
in length, per the general drain rule in the installation guide.

## 5. Loading and Detergent

Load plates and bowls facing the center spray arm on the lower rack. Use only detergent made for
automatic dishwashers; hand dish soap will oversud the unit and is not covered as a defect. Add
rinse aid using the universal rinse aid dispenser refill kit if the dispenser indicator shows
empty.

## 6. Maintenance and Filter Cleaning

The DW-300 uses a manual fine filter at the bottom of the tub, beneath the lower spray arm. Clean
this filter every 30 cycles by twisting it a quarter turn and rinsing it under a faucet. A
clogged filter is the most common cause of poor cleaning performance and slow draining.

## 7. Troubleshooting

If the control panel displays an error code, see the dishwasher error codes in
`troubleshooting.md`. Not every code in that table applies to this model: each entry lists the
models it covers. A code the table does not list for the DW-300 does not correspond to a real
condition on this unit and should be reported to service rather than acted on.

## 8. Replacement Parts

The DW-300 heating element is a 750W unit, part number HLV-4471. The DW-300 drain pump is part
number HLV-2201. The DW-300 door latch switch is part number HLV-7701. Current prices for these
parts are listed in `parts-list.md`.

## 9. Warranty and Safety

The DW-300 is covered by Halvorsen's standard warranty; see `warranty-policy.md` for full terms
and exclusions. Do not remove the rating label. Keep children away from detergent and the open
door during loading.
