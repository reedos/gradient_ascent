# Engineering Change Notice ECN-2608-04

Orbeck Power Systems. Issued 08/14/2026. Affects SRB-5030 revision A and revision B. Status:
approved and in effect. This notice changes one number in `srb5030-datasheet.md` revision B, which
has not been reissued.

## 1. Change

The maximum continuous input voltage of the SRB-5030 is **32.0 V** for board revisions A and B.

`srb5030-datasheet.md` revision B, section 3, states 36.0 V. That figure is superseded for
revisions A and B and remains correct only for revision C, which is not yet released. Where this
notice and the datasheet disagree, this notice governs for revisions A and B.

Nothing else changes. The output voltage, the current rating, the switching frequency, the ripple
limit and the efficiency figures are unaffected.

## 2. Reason

C1 and C2, the input capacitors, are 10 uF 50 V X7R parts, OPS-3105. Design review rule DR-14 in
`design-review-rules.md` requires a ceramic capacitor on a DC rail to be rated at least 1.5 times
the maximum steady-state rail voltage:

    50 V / 1.5 = 33.3 V

so a 50 V part supports a 33.3 V rail, not a 36.0 V one. At 36.0 V the derating factor is 50/36 =
1.39, short of the rule. The rule exists because an X7R part loses capacitance with DC bias and
because a ceramic that cracks fails short, on the input rail, with no fuse between it and the
source.

This was found during the revision C design review on 08/11/2026, not in the field. No field
failure has been attributed to it, and no board has been returned for it.

## 3. Disposition

| Population | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Boards in the field, revisions A and B | No recall. Notify customers with a 24 V nominal rail whose supply tolerance could reach 36 V. |
| Boards in stock, revision B | Relabel the box with the 32.0 V figure. No rework. |
| Boards in build, revision B | Ship with the revised label. |
| Revision C | Input capacitors change to 10 uF 63 V X7R, OPS-3106. 63 / 1.5 = 42 V, so 36.0 V is supported with margin. |

A 24 V industrial rail with a +/-10 percent tolerance reaches 26.4 V, well inside 32.0 V. The
change matters to customers running a 36 V nominal rail, which the datasheet permitted.

## 4. Test and Documentation Changes

`srb5030-test-spec.md` revision 3, dated 08/17/2026, changes step 4 from a 9.0 V to 36.0 V line
regulation sweep to a 9.0 V to 32.0 V sweep. Production may not apply 36.0 V to a revision A or
revision B board, including during test.

The datasheet will be reissued as revision C when board revision C is released. Until then the
datasheet and this notice disagree on the maximum input voltage, and anyone answering the question
"what is the maximum input voltage of an SRB-5030" has to ask which revision the board is, read
the revision off the silkscreen next to J1 or off the last character of the assembly number, and
answer accordingly:

- Revision A or B: 32.0 V, per this notice.
- Revision C: 36.0 V, per the datasheet, once released.
